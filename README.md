# 🌳 MindMirror — System Definition

MindMirror is a **Life Operating System** that enables users to organize, manage, and improve every aspect of their lives through a single, interactive **Mind Tree**. Instead of navigating traditional pages and menus, users interact with a living tree where the root represents their life, major branches represent life domains (such as Health, Learning, Career, and Personal), and sub-branches organize goals, skills, habits, projects, routines, notes, trackers, and achievements. Each branch can grow infinitely, allowing users to create a personalized structure that reflects their unique life journey.

The Mind Tree serves as the application's **primary dashboard, navigation system, and progress visualization**. Users can expand branches, manage content, complete habits, practice skills, track goals, record achievements, and monitor statistics directly within the tree. Every positive action contributes to the growth of the corresponding branch, providing a visual representation of personal development over time.

MindMirror follows a modular architecture where every life element is represented as a node within the tree. Nodes can contain child nodes, enabling unlimited hierarchy and flexible organization. This design allows users to customize their Life Tree according to their own priorities while maintaining a unified structure for navigation, tracking, analytics, and future expansion.

The ultimate goal of MindMirror is to transform personal management into a natural, visual, and engaging experience where users continuously build knowledge, develop skills, maintain healthy habits, achieve long-term goals, and watch their life evolve through a dynamic ecosystem that grows alongside them.

# Dashboard Design

The MindMirror Dashboard is designed as an interactive **Life Map**, inspired by the navigation experience of Google Maps. Instead of streets and cities, users explore their lives through a living **Mind Tree**. The dashboard begins with a single **Seed**, representing the user's life. As the user grows, the seed transforms into a sprout, then a young plant, and eventually a fully developed tree. Every major life area grows outward as a branch, while goals, skills, habits, projects, routines, and achievements appear as connected sub-branches.

Users navigate the dashboard using familiar map controls. They can **zoom in** to view detailed branches and individual nodes, **zoom out** for a complete overview of their life, **pan** by dragging the canvas in any direction, and **recenter** the view on the main Life Tree at any time. A floating toolbar provides quick actions such as search, create a new branch, expand or collapse branches, switch visualization modes, view notifications, access statistics, and open user settings. A minimap in the corner displays the entire tree and highlights the current viewing area, making navigation easy even as the tree becomes very large.

The visual theme is entirely nature-inspired. The background resembles a calm natural environment, while branches grow organically from the center. Healthy and active branches appear vibrant and flourishing, whereas neglected branches may appear faded until activity resumes. Every completed habit, practiced skill, achieved goal, or finished project contributes to the growth of the corresponding branch, making the dashboard a real-time visualization of the user's personal development. Rather than simply displaying data, the dashboard tells the story of the user's life as a living plant that continuously grows, evolves, and reflects their progress.
